SOUNDSPHERE / NASA EARTH OBSERVATIONS

Explore the living Earth.

Switch between NASA satellite layers and explore how our planet changes. These are scientific visualizations, not decorative heat effects.

01 / LAND

Land heat map

Daytime land-surface temperature observations from the MODIS instrument aboard NASA’s Terra satellite. Clouds and missing observations may leave gaps.

02 / FIRE

Wildfire activity

Thermal-anomaly detections from NASA’s MODIS instruments. A detection indicates a possible active fire or other hot source; it is not a burned-area boundary.

03 / OCEAN

Ocean temperature

Daily sea-surface temperature analysis from the GHRSST MUR product distributed through NASA GIBS / JPL PO.DAAC.

04 / ICE

Sea ice

Sea-ice observations from MODIS Terra. This visualization is not the NSIDC Sea Ice Index and does not use NOAA data.

05 / WIND DATASET

Ocean winds · NASA PO.DAAC

NASA’s ASCAT ocean-surface wind-vector dataset is available from PO.DAAC. It is listed as a verified NASA data source, but is not presented as a GIBS map layer in this version.
